# Student Start Here

You are in **AI Scholars Club** — a three-year G10–G12 pathway to become a thoughtful AI practitioner with a real portfolio.

Your teacher assigns **one year band, one session, and one evidence task at a time**.

## Your Route

| Year | What you build |
|---|---|
| **G10 (Year A)** | Data skills, classical ML, ethics, first scholar project |
| **G11 (Year B)** | PyTorch, computer vision, robotics vision, applied demo |
| **G12 (Year C)** | Advanced AI, responsible deployment, capstone showcase |

Full sequence: [Club Pathway G10–G12](00_Course_Overview/Club_Pathway_G10-G12.md)

## Before You Start

You should have completed:

- **G8** — Software Design, Development & AI (Python, web, HCI, GitHub, AI literacy)
- **G9** — AP Computer Science Principles

If you are missing pieces, Session 3 is a **diagnostic** — your teacher will assign short gap-fill, not a full repeat of earlier grades.

## Every Class

```text
Skill Warm-up → Talk Robin 1 → Entry Check → Core Pattern
→ Guided Practice → Independent Rebuild → Talk Robin 2 + Evidence
```

You learn by **rebuilding and explaining**, not by copying once.

## What You Keep

Maintain a **portfolio** with:

- notebooks and code you can rebuild
- model cards and error analyses
- ethics briefs and AI disclosure notes
- project presentations and peer feedback

## Competitions

NOAI, IOAI, and Kaggle are **optional** — extra sessions for students who want contest prep after Year B. They are **not required** to be an AI Scholar.
