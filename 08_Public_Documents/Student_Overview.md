# Student Overview

**AI Scholars Club** is a three-year G10–G12 pathway. You build ML skills, responsible-AI judgment, computer vision (including robotics), and a portfolio capstone.

## What you do

- learn through short resources, partner discussion, guided practice, and **independent rebuild**
- keep a **portfolio** of projects, model cards, ethics briefs, and presentations
- connect AI to **real applications** — including camera/robotics demos in Year B

## What this is not

- not a contest cram course (competitions are optional add-ons)
- not “watch a video and copy code” — you must explain and rebuild your work
- not a repeat of G8/G9 — you start with a diagnostic, not ten weeks of Python basics

## Three years at a glance

| Year | You build |
|---|---|
| G10 | Data + classical ML + ethics + first project |
| G11 | PyTorch + computer vision + robotics vision |
| G12 | Advanced AI + capstone showcase |
