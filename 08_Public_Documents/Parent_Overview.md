# Parent Overview

**AI Scholars Club** is a three-year G10–G12 programme on the school's AI specialization track. Students build machine-learning literacy, responsible-AI judgment, computer vision skills (including robotics applications), and a portfolio capstone suitable for college and internship applications.

## What students gain

- reproducible data and modeling workflows
- ethics and AI-literacy discussions grounded in real cases
- applied projects with presentations and peer review
- optional competition preparation for motivated students (NOAI, IOAI, Kaggle) — **not required** for programme completion

## How progress is measured

Progress is shown through **portfolio evidence** — code students can rebuild, written analyses, model cards, ethics briefs, and showcase presentations — not attendance alone.

## Prerequisites

Students are expected to have completed **G8 Software Design, Development & AI** and **G9 AP Computer Science Principles** before joining the club.

## Time commitment

Roughly one 75-minute after-school session per week across three academic years (72 core sessions), with modest reading or notebook work between sessions during project phases.
