# Session 52 — Support Vector Machines

## Goal

Understand margins, support points, scaling, and the geometric intuition of SVMs.

## Learn

Interpret a separating boundary and margin. Explain why points near the boundary matter most and why scaling affects geometry.

## Practice

- sketch a maximum-margin boundary for a tiny 2D example;
- identify support points;
- compare scaled vs unscaled behaviour;
- compare linear SVM with a linear/logistic baseline.

## Rebuild

Fit an SVM on a suitable small dataset under a fixed validation protocol. Students must state whether a linear or non-linear kernel is justified before trying it.

## Share

Discuss accuracy, margin, runtime, interpretability, and the risk of blindly increasing model flexibility.

## Evidence

- margin/support-point sketch;
- scaling note;
- model comparison;
- kernel-choice rationale.

## Exit Check

Student can explain the role of the margin/support points and identify when SVM complexity or scaling requirements matter.
