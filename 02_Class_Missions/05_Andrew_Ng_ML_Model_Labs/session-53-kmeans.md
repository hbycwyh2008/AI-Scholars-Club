# Session 53 — K-Means Clustering

## Goal

Understand centroid assignment/update and separate “found clusters” from “useful real-world groups.”

## Learn

Define unsupervised clustering, squared distance to centroids, assignment step, update step, and sensitivity to scale/initialisation/`k`.

## Practice

- assign a few points to centroids;
- recompute one centroid update;
- compare clustering before/after scaling;
- inspect multiple choices of `k` without pretending one score proves semantic truth.

## Rebuild

Students cluster a small unlabeled dataset, visualise or summarise the groups, and write what additional evidence would be needed to claim the clusters are meaningful.

## Share

Challenge a classmate’s cluster interpretation: is it supported by data, or merely a story attached after fitting?

## Evidence

- one assignment/update calculation;
- clustering notebook;
- interpretation with limitation;
- scaling/`k` decision note.

## Exit Check

Student can perform one K-means iteration and explain why clustering quality and real-world usefulness are separate questions.
