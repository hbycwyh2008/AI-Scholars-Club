## Phase Gate

On an unfamiliar task, students identify `X`, `y`, label availability, task family, validation design, baseline, metric, candidate models, and likely failure modes. They perform at least one representative mathematical trace, implement a defensible baseline, analyse errors, and justify the next controlled experiment.
