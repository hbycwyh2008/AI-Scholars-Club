# 01 — AI History and Thinking Humans

**Current IOAI core pathway:** first substantive unit after orientation  
**Core text:** Melanie Mitchell, *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*  
**Suggested length:** 8 × 70–75 minutes

## Why This Comes First

Students build the AI conceptual map **before** the CodeHS Python/data units. They should first understand the major AI schools, the historical shift from symbolic methods to learning-based systems, the relationship among AI / ML / deep learning / CV / NLP / RL, and the limits of benchmark success.

The current order is intentional:

```text
Course Introduction / Orientation
→ AI History and Thinking Humans
→ CodeHS Data Science with Python
→ CodeHS Advanced Python and AI Programming
→ ML Workflow Bootcamp
```

## Start Here

[**Open the AI History lesson launcher**](SESSION_LAUNCHER.md)

The launcher provides the reading range, seminar lesson, and required evidence for all eight seminars.

## Purpose

Use eight reading-and-reasoning seminars to build historical perspective, conceptual boundaries, claim auditing, evidence-based argument, and critical judgement before students specialise in tools and models.

Students complete the assigned reading before class. The book is not copied into the repository; students use a legally obtained copy.

During this unit, use light problem-framing questions rather than forcing model vocabulary too early:

```text
What problem is being solved?
What information goes in?
What output counts as success?
What evidence would convince us that the system works?
```
