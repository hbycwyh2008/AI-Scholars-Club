**Scheduled sessions:** 1–2

## Purpose

Begin with the reason for the course, not with tools. Session 1 introduces NOAI, IOAI, their competition stages and content, the relationship between the national and international pathways, the course learning routes, and the evidence students must eventually produce. Session 2 then establishes the student repository, evidence system, Bohrium access, Jupyter environment, and responsible-AI routine before CS50P begins.

## Gate

By the end of Phase 0, students can explain the relationship between NOAI and IOAI, compare the main competition stages and capability demands, state why this course is structured from foundations to competition execution, identify their assigned route, create and use their repository, run Python, locate assigned resources, and submit reproducible evidence without copying a complete solution.
